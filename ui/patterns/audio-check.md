# Real audio comprehension
**Use:** learner listens to actually accessible audio then answers gist/detail questions.
**Input:** real playable recorded/spoken source with controls, plus concise options or short answers.
**Hints:** replay or point to gist, not a written transcript that gives away the entire answer.
**Feedback:** cite what was and wasn't understood; don't treat reading of a transcript as listening.
**Evidence:** audio comprehension only if playback was actually available.
**Fallback:** a text reading task, clearly labelled reading; listening stays unassessed.
