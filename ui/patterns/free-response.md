# Free typed response
**Use:** elicit independent explanation, short writing, reading paraphrase or authentic message.
**Input:** labelled textarea with concise scope and realistic situation.
**Hints:** task goal → sentence frame → sample only after response; record hint level.
**Feedback:** prioritize successful communication and at most two corrections.
**Evidence:** typed production, interaction or reading comprehension as specified by trusted rubric. Never call it audio or speaking.
**Fallback:** ordinary chat reply carrying the same task ID.
