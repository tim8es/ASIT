# Community code of conduct

ASIT contributors, reviewers and maintainers should collaborate professionally and respectfully.

We expect constructive technical feedback, clear descriptions of problems, consideration for different language proficiency levels, and respect for privacy and accessibility. Disagreement is welcome; personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, threats, repeated disruption and publication of private information are not.

Maintainership may edit or remove abusive comments, close disruptive threads or restrict participation when needed. Report serious behavior concerns privately to the maintainers through their GitHub profiles; do not post private reports publicly.

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews and project-related community spaces.
