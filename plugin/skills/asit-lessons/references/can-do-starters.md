# Optional can-do prompts — migrated from illustrative legacy curriculum

This is an **illustrative** starting catalog, not a default learner plan, curriculum placement test, or verified CEFR descriptor mapping. Never preseed a learner workbook or award CEFR levels based on these examples.

- INTERACT-GREET: Open and close a simple conversation appropriately
- INTERACT-CLARIFY: Ask for repetition or clarification when meaning is unclear
- INTERACT-OPINION: Express an opinion and support it with a reason
- NARRATE-PAST: Describe a past experience in sequence
- PLAN-FUTURE: Describe plans and likely next steps
- READ-GIST: Identify the main point of a clear short text
- LISTEN-GIST: Identify gist from actually heard speech
- WRITE-MESSAGE: Write a clear short message for the intended audience
- EXPLAIN-TRADEOFF: Compare options and explain trade-offs (may depend on INTERACT-OPINION)
- SUSTAIN-DISCUSSION: Maintain connected conversation on familiar topics (may depend on INTERACT-CLARIFY and INTERACT-OPINION)

Select and validate dependencies for each real learner's goals. Task-level CEFR alignment requires a verified official descriptor and its source, separately from this list.
