# Genuine ASIT Voice and multi-turn conversation

Use the host's **actual Voice mode** when it is available and the learner wants live speech. The in-chat plugin or native lesson form **cannot activate Voice, microphone or recording programmatically**; avoid nonfunctional Play/Record widgets. Instruct the learner to tap the platform Voice icon in the **current chat** and grant microphone permission if requested; then proceed with the requested goal. Do not promise availability for all plans/clients.

## Live lesson paths

- **Listening:** give 1–3 short level-appropriate spoken sentences **after Voice is actually active and audible**, then ask one gist and one detail question, listen to response and follow up. If the user relied on on-screen transcript, label audio+text support. If voice didn't play, listening is `not_assessed`.
- **Speaking and dialogue:** set a real communicative situation, ask a learner to reply, respond to the actual content and ask a second follow-up. At least **two** genuine learner replies for sustained dialogue evidence. Typed role-play counts only as written interaction.
- **Listen and repeat / shadowing:** provide an audible model in the active Voice session and invite learner repetition; only evaluate pronunciation/intonation if the assessing teacher actually has access to the **original audio**, not just an approximate transcript. Otherwise phrase oral practice as rehearsal and mark pronunciation `not_assessed`.
- **Self-paced prerecorded audio:** when user wants Play → Replay → answer instead of live conversation, use `asit-media`, not a fake Voice wrapper.

## Capabilities and evidence

Voice may show text/subtitles; hidden unseen listening can't be guaranteed. Ask whether learner actually heard and whether they read text; grade content vs oral evidence accordingly. Transcript can be nonverbatim, so don't claim timing, phonemes, accent or fluency speed from it. If Voice is missing or not activated, offer explicitly text-based role-play/reading without calling it audible.

No automatic saving of raw audio. Consent for audio and transcripts separate from ordinary written summaries; no uploads to GitHub. Finish with concise actual evidence and one retry opportunity.
