Parcel Mission

Mission: Predict where the parcel will be, move it, check your idea, then ask a new English question. This is a diagram, not a photograph.

1. Predict, then move

The parcel begins on the shelf. The courier will move it to the container on the right. Look at the scene, predict its final position, and press Move parcel.

Where do you think it will land?

2. Use it elsewhere

Your colleague is looking for their phone. Write one original question asking where the phone is. This is a different situation; no model question is supplied.

Standalone browser demo. No analytics, network requests, audio, timer, CEFR rating, Google Sheets connection or ASIT_ATTEMPT_V2 Submit. An original text answer is not graded by this page.
